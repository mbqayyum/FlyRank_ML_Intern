## 4. Key Limitations & Operational Boundaries
1. **Observational Association, Not Direct Causality:** High model scores indicate statistical patterns associated with organic search decline; updating an article does not automatically guarantee rank recovery.
2. **No Black-Box Algorithm Prediction:** The model evaluates lagging observable search performance metrics, not unannounced search engine ranking updates.
3. **Strict Non-Automation Guardrails:** Never automate destructive editorial operations (page deletion, programmatic redirects, unreviewed AI content regeneration).
